About Financial Clarity, specifically

Is this the same as bookkeeping?

No. Bookkeeping records transactions. Financial Clarity interprets what those numbers actually mean and what to do about them.

Do I need existing accounting software or records?

Whatever you currently have is the starting point — part of the engagement is getting your financial picture into a state that's actually usable.

Why is there a three-month minimum?

Financial clarity compounds. One month of numbers tells you very little; a consistent rhythm over several months is what actually changes decisions.

Can I combine this with LIFT or Scale?

Yes — Financial Clarity often runs alongside another engagement, especially where financial confusion is part of a larger operational picture The Scan identifies.
