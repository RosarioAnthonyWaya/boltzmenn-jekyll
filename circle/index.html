FAQ

About Circle, specifically

If there are no tiers, how is Circle priced?

Scope first, price second. We agree exactly what ongoing support looks like for your business, then price is set around that scope in writing.

Do I need to go through The Scan first?

Yes. Circle is for businesses we already understand through The Scan and often a prior engagement — not a cold entry point.

Is Circle capacity really limited?

Yes, deliberately. Availability is capped to protect the quality of attention every Circle client gets — it isn't a scalable retainer sold without limit.

Can I move from LIFT or Scale into Circle?

That's the most common path — once a structural engagement is complete, some clients want the same team to stay involved long-term.
